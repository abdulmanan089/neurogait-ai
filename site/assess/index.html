Clinical assessment workspace

Non-invasive motor, vocal and eye-movement screening in about five minutes.

Webcam hand tracking quantifies rest tremor and finger-tapping bradykinesia. A sustained vowel yields jitter, shimmer and HNR. Both are combined into one transparent screening score.

Motor vision

21-point hand landmarks, 3–7 Hz tremor band, tap speed, decrement, rhythm.

Vocal biomarkers

Cycle-to-cycle F0, jitter, shimmer and harmonics-to-noise ratio.

Decision support

Weighted score with feature contributions and normative comparison.

Report & export

Printable report, PDF via print dialog, and raw JSON data.

Research screening tool — not a medical diagnosis. NeuroGait AI produces preliminary, experimental indicators only. Results must not be used to diagnose, exclude, or treat any condition and should be interpreted by a qualified clinician.
